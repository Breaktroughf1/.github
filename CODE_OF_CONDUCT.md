# Code of conduct

This applies to every BreaktroughF1 space on GitHub: repositories, issues, pull requests and Discussions.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. People of every background and experience level are welcome.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Help others learn. A beginner's question is a fair question.
- Stay on topic, and keep anything that isn't about our projects out of these spaces.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, advertising or repeated off-topic posts
- Posting security vulnerabilities in public. Report them privately instead (see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md)).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove posts, lock discussions, and block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem, email **info@breaktroughf1.com**; reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
